Delete your Taskdot account

This removes your account and the data listed below. It cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Taskdot and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm, then choose Delete.

If you cannot sign in

Email ak.hansson88@gmail.com from the email address on the account with the subject “Delete my Taskdot account”. We will verify that you own the account and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, nickname, email address, role and approval status.
  • Your presence and online status.
  • Tasks you own, with their comments and attachments.
  • Your companion connections and friend requests.
  • Your direct-message threads, messages and their attachments.
  • Your notifications.

What remains

Messages and tasks you sent to other people may remain in their copy of a conversation or on a task they own. Backup copies are removed within 30 days. We may keep a minimal record where the law requires it.
