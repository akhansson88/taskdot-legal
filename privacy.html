Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Taskdot is a shared task board for invited teams. This policy explains what the Taskdot apps and the Taskdot web app collect, why, where it is stored and how you can delete it.

Who runs Taskdot

Taskdot is operated by Alexander Hansson, Gothenburg, Sweden. Contact: ak.hansson88@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, nickname, user ID, role and approval status.
  • Content you create: tasks, comments, attachments, direct messages and companion connections.
  • Presence: whether you are online, inactive or offline, and when that last changed.
  • Technical data: standard server logs from our hosting and database providers, including IP address, device and browser type, and error information.

We do not use advertising, tracking pixels or third-party analytics, and we do not sell personal data.

Why we use it

To create and secure your account, show the board and messages to the people on your team, keep the service working, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Where it is stored

Account data, tasks, comments and messages are stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database, European Union region). Attachment files are stored in Supabase. The web app and its server functions run on Netlify. These providers process data on our behalf under their standard data-processing terms.

Data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS.

Who can see it

Only approved members of your Taskdot team can see the board, companions and messages inside that team. The team administrator can approve or reject accounts. Our service providers process the data to run the service. We do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and content while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, presence, tasks you own, companion connections, direct messages and attachment files are deleted. Backup copies are removed within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: open Profile and choose Delete account. You will be asked to sign in again, then the account and its data are removed.
  • Delete by request: use the account deletion page or email ak.hansson88@gmail.com from the address on the account.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, correct your nickname or email, or ask a question about this policy at the same address.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

Taskdot is a workplace tool for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will be announced in the app.
