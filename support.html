Support

For members of a Taskdot team

Email ak.hansson88@gmail.com and include the email address on your Taskdot account, the app (Android or iPhone), and what happened. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

I created an account but cannot see the board

New accounts wait for the team administrator to approve them. Ask your Taskdot admin to approve your account, then sign in again.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen choose Forgot password. A reset link is sent to the email address on the account.

I need to delete my account

Open Profile in the app and choose Delete account, or follow the steps on the account deletion page.

Attachments will not upload

Check your connection and try a smaller file. Supported files are images, PDFs, documents, text and small archives. If it still fails, email us with the file type and size.

Messages or the board are not updating

Pull down to refresh, then close and reopen the app. If you are offline, changes are applied when the connection returns. If the problem continues, tell us the time and which screen.
